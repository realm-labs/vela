# Required trait signature parameters

B04.11 targets 32 positive/negative service/protocol partitions for references,
highlight, prepare-rename and rename under S4/S5. Their requirement IDs follow
`<feature>/syntax/<S4|S5>/<positive|negative>/<service|protocol>`. Broad cells
remain unreviewed until other ownership and lifecycle partitions are complete.

The independent fixture has 19 query points and five groups: required signatures, sibling signatures,
same-named traits, concrete implementations and caller values. A required
parameter appears in two distinct default-expression binding maps. Queries and
applied edits must preserve its declaration identity across both defaults and
all owned named labels, including aliases, closures, recovery and closed files.
Unknown labels/receivers and strings are negative cases; existing-parameter and
owned unknown-label capture must reject without affecting sibling signatures.
Renaming to a local variable inside one default expression must also reject.

Validation uses the shared service and protocol coordinate drivers: exact sets,
kinds, highlights, prepare/definition targets, full edit plans, actual application,
whole-file comparison, parsing, repeated queries and restoration. Protocol also
checks LF/CRLF, UTF-16, encoded paths and open/closed versions. This child does
not certify schema callable ownership or close broad S4/S5 cells.

Resolved call-label collection is shared with body-backed methods and tuple
variants. Required signatures use the canonical method and parameter spans;
references and collision checks visit every default binding map. Local rename
implementation is extracted into its own module to keep the parent reviewable.
Original B00-B03 snapshots remain fixed, Windows and macOS evidence stay separate,
and B16 remains deferred.
